## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-12

### Added

- Generic stage-wise ADOPT-FPML optimizer with four interaction configurations.
- Replaceable FP model, ML trainer, selector, objective, thresholds, parameter-target generator, and pruning policies.
- E1 and E2 synthetic data generators and portable pretrained reference configurations.
- Bundled training/held-out data, reproducibility tests, CLI, examples, documentation, CI, and trusted PyPI publishing workflow.
